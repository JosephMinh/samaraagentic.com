Access is limited to the status described below; there is no general AI-processing authorization. This website is informational only and does not grant Gmail access.

Active access boundary

Access is limited.

The active Google permissions are exactly https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send, openid, email, and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email.

Important: the Gmail read-only scope can technically view all messages, settings, and attachments in the account. The Gmail send scope can technically send email as the account. These capabilities are not limited to a chosen sender or a selected subset of messages.

The stored authorization does not include Gmail modify, compose, or full-mail scopes. The Gmail send permission is a technical capability, not blanket authority: its authorized use is limited to the active self-only service described above. Sending to Crous or any third party, mailbox changes, provider-side reply drafts, and unrestricted monitoring are not authorized. The service does not promise future delivery, coverage, response time, or quota availability.
