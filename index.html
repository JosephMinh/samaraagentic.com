Active access boundary

Access is read-only.

The active Google permissions are https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly, openid, email, and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email.

Important: the Gmail read-only scope can technically view all messages, settings, and attachments in the selected account. It is not limited to a chosen sender or a selected subset of messages.

That scope does not permit sending mail or changing mail. Sending, mailbox changes, provider-side reply drafts, and unrestricted monitoring are not authorized. Monitoring beyond the completed bounded trial, or any automatic response, would require separate, explicit authorization and appropriate permissions.
