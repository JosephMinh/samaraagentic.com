Privacy & access

Limited read-only access is active.

This is an information page for a one-person Gmail assistant. It is not a sign-in page and it cannot grant access to Gmail.

Current status

One personal account has active Gmail read-only and identity authorization, with a local token. One specifically approved exchange was inspected, and selected excerpts were processed by the assistant’s external OpenAI Codex model provider for that one task.

Historical attempt 1 stopped before identity binding with zero sends. Historical attempt 2 sent one synthetic seed, later reconciled as sent and inbox mail with exact-self routing, and had zero replies.

On October 1, 2026, one fresh bounded self-only functional test sent and inspected three synthetic seeds. Two self-only replies were accepted, each on its corresponding seed thread; an automatic-response decoy received no reply. The test ended at its reply cap with no active dispatch or retry. This was not authorization for unrestricted monitoring.

Active permissions

The active scopes are:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly
  • openid
  • email
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email

The read-only Gmail scope can technically view all messages, settings, and attachments in the selected account. It does not permit sending, deleting, labeling, archiving, marking mail read, or otherwise changing mail.

Google describes Gmail scopes in its Gmail API scope guide.

Intended purpose

The completed authorized uses are limited to inspecting one specifically approved exchange, processing selected excerpts for that task, and the dated, bounded self-only test described above. It is functional self-only proof only: it does not establish general or real Crous coverage, independent post-send inbox delivery for every new message, a complete MIME audit, or guaranteed receive-to-reply latency. The recorded local elapsed time is not Gmail latency.

No ongoing sending, provider-side reply drafts, or unrestricted monitoring is authorized. Real-recipient, Crous, and third-party sends remain unauthorized; any further monitoring, response, or send would require separate, explicit authorization and appropriate permissions.

What has not been finalized

There is no settled public disclosure yet for where mail-related data would be stored, how long it would be retained, how deletion would work, or how backups and logs would be handled.

The existence of a local token does not settle token storage, unlock, security, or rotation details. Apart from the one approved task, general future AI use, provider handling, data handling, log retention, and which message excerpts could be sent to a provider are not finalized.

Public contact information has not been supplied for publication. These unresolved items need owner and provider review before any further mailbox use.

Future revocation

The account owner can revoke the active grant through Google Account third-party connections. Removing the local token would be a separate step.
