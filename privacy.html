Privacy & access

Limited Gmail access is active.

This is an information page for a one-person Gmail assistant. It is not a sign-in page and it cannot grant access to Gmail.

Current status

One personal account has active Gmail read-only, Gmail send, and identity authorization, with a local token. One specifically approved exchange was inspected, and selected excerpts were processed by the assistant’s external OpenAI Codex model provider for that task.

A limited ongoing reply service is active only for qualifying mail sent by that same account. This is not general sending authority or authorization for unrestricted monitoring.

Earlier limited tests and failures are historical and do not describe the current service.

Active permissions

The active scopes are exactly:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send
  • openid
  • email
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email

The Gmail read-only scope can technically view all messages, settings, and attachments in the account. The Gmail send scope can technically send email as the account. The stored authorization does not include Gmail modify, compose, or full-mail scopes.

Google describes Gmail scopes in its Gmail API scope guide.

Intended purpose

The completed authorized uses are limited to inspecting one specifically approved exchange and processing selected excerpts for that task. Active authorization is limited to the ongoing self-only reply service for qualifying mail sent by that same account. The Gmail send capability is not blanket authority: sending to Crous or any third party, mailbox mutation, provider-side reply drafts, and unrestricted monitoring remain unauthorized. The service does not promise future delivery, coverage, response time, or quota availability.

What has not been finalized

There is no settled public disclosure yet for where mail-related data would be stored, how long it would be retained, how deletion would work, or how backups and logs would be handled.

The existence of a local token does not settle token storage, unlock, security, or rotation details. Apart from the one approved task, general future AI use, provider handling, data handling, log retention, and which message excerpts could be sent to a provider are not finalized.

Public contact information has not been supplied for publication. These unresolved items need owner and provider review before any expansion of mailbox use.

Future revocation

The account owner can revoke the active grant through Google Account third-party connections. Removing the local token would be a separate step.
